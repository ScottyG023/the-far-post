Who We Are

Our story.

A modern licensed club, built for the Northern Beaches football community.

The Far Post is a modern Licensed Club that has been developed for the Northern Beaches football and broader communities. Located at Cromer Park, the site was redeveloped in 2021 so that patrons could experience a relaxing, friendly atmosphere while watching football or enjoying the company of friends and teammates.

We offer a modern bar that caters to a wide variety of tastes, with five draught beers and ginger beer on tap, plus a selection of bottled beer, wine, spirits and soft drinks. Whether you're looking for a traditional drink or a more modern XPA, The Far Post has a drink for everyone.

Meanwhile, our bistro offers a broad range of food, catered for by award-winning caterer Silver Pantry.

The showpiece of The Far Post is the large, sun-drenched deck that offers a relaxed outdoor setting for both meals and drinks. Our deck has a full view of Cromer Park's No. 1 synthetic field — home to both Manly United Football Club and a wide range of Manly Warringah FA community matches.
